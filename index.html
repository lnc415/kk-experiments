Our Story

It all started on a quiet Sunday morning with flour dusting the countertops, the smell of butter melting in a warm oven, and a kitchen full of love. The Copper Whisk was never meant to be a business — it was simply a way of sharing the thing that brought our family the most joy.

Every loaf, every roll, every cookie is made entirely by hand — from scratch, with care, using recipes that have been folded and re-folded over years of Sunday mornings and birthday celebrations. We bake in small batches because we believe the best things in life can't be rushed.

Whether you're treating yourself after a long week or bringing something warm to the table for someone you love, we hope every bite feels like coming home.

— The Copper Whisk Family
